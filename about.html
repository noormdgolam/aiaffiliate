Method & sources

What the instruments are, what they are not, and where every claim on this site comes from.

What this is

Seven classical problems in the philosophy of mind, rebuilt as procedures rather than prose. The wager is that an argument you have undergone sits differently from one you have read — that discovering the hole in your own visual field is a different kind of event from being told it is there.

What it is not

It is not research. You are one participant, self-administering, unblinded, aware of the hypothesis, with no control group and no baseline. Nothing an instrument reports about you personally should be taken as a measurement of you.

What is solid is the published work each instrument is modelled on, cited at the foot of every page. The instruments are demonstrations of established effects, not tests of them.

Three of the seven have known interpretive controversies, and in each case the objections are given on the page rather than tucked away: Libet’s clock has a serious methodological challenge in Schurger’s accumulator model; Searle’s room has never recovered from the Systems Reply, depending on whom you ask; and the trolley pair in Instrument VI is not a framing error, whatever the surrounding items are doing, and is presented as a different kind of case.

The deception, disclosed

Instrument III substitutes your choice on two of five trials and asks you to justify a preference you did not express. This is the choice-blindness paradigm of Johansson and colleagues, and it does not work if announced in advance — which is why the site tells you beforehand that something will be done and reveals precisely what within about ninety seconds.

If you would rather not be deceived at all, skip Instrument III; the ledger works without it. Nothing you type there is transmitted anywhere.

Data

There is no server, no database, no account, no cookie, no analytics, no telemetry, and no third-party request of any kind. The site makes no network calls at all after the page loads — it will run from a USB stick on a machine that has never been online.

Your results are written to localStorage under a single key on your own device, so that the ledger can assemble them across pages. The erase button on the ledger removes that key entirely. Clearing your browser’s site data does the same thing.

Construction

Static HTML, one stylesheet, and hand-written JavaScript with no dependencies, no build step, and no framework. All figures are drawn to <canvas> at runtime; there is not a single image file in the project. Type is set in whatever serif and monospace your system provides, so nothing is fetched and nothing is tracked.

Each instrument is one HTML file plus one script. If you want to see exactly what an instrument does to you, the script is short enough to read in a few minutes, and reading it is encouraged.

A note on tone

The findings address you directly and sometimes bluntly. That is deliberate — the whole point is that these are not facts about people in general but events that just happened to the person reading. None of it is meant as a claim that you are foolish. Every effect demonstrated here is universal, appears in experts on their own subject matter, and persists undiminished in people who know all about it. Knowing about the blind spot does not close it.
